About Me

Hi, I’m Krishna, an electrical engineering and physics student at Penn. My focus is circuits and hardware systems, spanning analog and mixed-signal design, digital hardware, memory and emerging semiconductor devices. I’m interested in how these pieces come together in complete systems.

My work includes sensing ASICs, clock generation, digital accelerators, camera systems at Qualcomm, and ferroelectric device research at Penn. I also work on hardware/software codesign and embedded interfaces, and build software and ML tools for inference, profiling and verification.

When I'm not debugging simulations or in the lab, you'll usually find me playing tennis or watching far too much YouTube when I should be sleeping :D
