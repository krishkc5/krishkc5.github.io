About Me

Hi, I’m Krishna, an electrical engineering and physics student at Penn. I design analog and digital circuits, build embedded systems, and study ferroelectric devices for compute-in-memory.

At Qualcomm, I worked on camera synchronization across drivers, middleware and hardware timing. My current work includes AGRASIC, a mixed-signal agricultural sensing chip, analog design for an in-memory compute startup, and device research in the Jariwala Lab.

When I'm not debugging simulations or in the lab, you'll usually find me playing tennis or watching far too much YouTube when I should be sleeping :D
